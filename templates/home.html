Plastic Debris

Plastic debris refers to discarded plastic items such as bottles, bags, and packaging that pollute the environment. Due to its durability and resistance to natural degradation, plastic debris poses a serious threat to both land and marine ecosystems. It can take hundreds of years to decompose, causing long-term harm to wildlife and the environment.

Reducing plastic debris requires a collective effort through better waste management, recycling programs, and the reduction of single-use plastics. Innovative detection systems, such as AI-based recognition, can help identify and manage plastic waste more efficiently, reducing its impact on the environment and ensuring a cleaner, safer planet for future generations.

Glass Debris

Glass debris refers to broken or discarded glass objects, such as bottles, jars, and windows, that pose significant environmental and safety hazards. Glass takes an extremely long time to degrade and can harm wildlife and ecosystems if not managed properly. Sharp fragments can also be dangerous for humans and animals.

Addressing the issue of glass debris involves recycling and reusing glass materials, as they are 100% recyclable without loss of quality. Advanced detection systems can help identify glass debris in waste streams, ensuring efficient collection and processing. Together, we can minimize its impact and create safer, cleaner environments.

Textile Debris

Textile debris refers to discarded fabrics, fibers, and garments that often end up polluting our environment. This type of waste is a significant contributor to land and marine pollution due to its synthetic composition and slow decomposition rates. Addressing textile debris is crucial for mitigating its environmental impact and preserving ecosystems.

By adopting sustainable practices such as recycling and repurposing, we can reduce the accumulation of textile waste. Innovative detection and sorting systems are also pivotal in identifying and processing textile debris efficiently, paving the way for a cleaner and greener future.

Marine Debris Detection Using Improved YOLO

Our Marine Debris Detection System leverages the YOLO (You Only Look Once) algorithm to deliver real-time, accurate identification of marine litter in diverse aquatic environments.
